# Privacy Policy — Paisa Aayo

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Paisa Aayo, developed by jrtilak, helps you track payment notifications and hear payment announcements.

**Information we access**
With your permission, the app reads Android notifications to identify payments, including bank and wallet alerts shown by messaging apps. This may include notification text, sender details, amounts, transaction references, and timestamps. The app does not directly read your SMS inbox.

**How we use and store it**
We use this information for payment history, announcements, and troubleshooting. Notification data, payment records, and preferences are stored locally in the app’s private storage. Voice announcements work offline. Payment details may appear in notifications or be spoken aloud according to your settings.

**Sharing**
We do not automatically upload or sell your notification or payment data. If you send information to support, you choose what to share. Information sent to support is used to handle your request.

**Your control and deletion**
You can revoke notification access in Android settings at any time. You can delete payment history in the app. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes all locally stored app data.

**Changes and contact**
We may update this policy as the app changes.

Questions: contact@jrtilak.dev
